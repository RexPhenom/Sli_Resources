Our story

Smart technology should make everyday life simpler.

At SLI RESOURCES, we light up the future through innovation and smart technology. We specialise in developing cutting-edge automation products that improve everyday life in homes, farms and industries.

Our commitment is to create scalable and sustainable technology that empowers Africa and beyond. We work closely with clients to transform ideas into reliable, market-ready products, with a focus on quality and user-friendly design.

What we build

Our flagship product, Hydrolynk, is an automatic water pump controller that prevents dry running and flooding by monitoring water levels continuously. We also deliver products such as rechargeable whiteboards with erasers and IR blasters, and we offer consultancy in product innovation, prototyping and manufacturing.

Whether it is for personal or industrial use, our aim is the same: to automate, simplify and improve how people interact with technology.
